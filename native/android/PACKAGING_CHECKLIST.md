# Android Packaging Checklist

- Confirm final package id.
- Confirm production/PWA URL.
- Create reproducible wrapper/build configuration.
- Keep signing keystore outside the repository.
- Validate deep links, install/update and offline/error states.
- Validate privacy/data-safety disclosures.
- Generate signed AAB/APK only in an authorized environment.
- Test on real Android devices.
- Submit to Google Play and record review/publication evidence.

**Status:** preparation only; no signed Android package is claimed.
