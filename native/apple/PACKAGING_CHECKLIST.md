# Apple Packaging Checklist

- Confirm final bundle id.
- Confirm production/PWA URL.
- Configure authorized Apple developer account and signing outside the repository.
- Validate WebView/native shell behavior, safe areas and navigation.
- Validate privacy disclosures and account/login requirements.
- Build and test through an authorized Apple toolchain/TestFlight.
- Test on iPhone/iPad devices.
- Submit to App Store and record review/publication evidence.

**Status:** preparation only; no signed iOS/iPadOS package is claimed.
