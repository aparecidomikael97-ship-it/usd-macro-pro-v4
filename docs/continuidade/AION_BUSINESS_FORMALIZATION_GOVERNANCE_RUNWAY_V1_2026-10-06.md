# AION Business Formalization & Governance Runway V1

Status: DESIGN-ONLY / GUIDED FORMALIZATION / NO LEGAL OR TAX FILING EXECUTION

## Objective

Guide HUMAN_OWNER through the formal creation, protection and operation of the business behind AtlasQuant/AION without requiring prior legal, accounting or administrative expertise.

AION must teach each step, explain why it exists, show what evidence is required, and stop when specialist validation is necessary.

## Guided stages

1. BUSINESS_ACTIVITY_VALIDATION
2. CNAE_AND_TAX_FRAMING_REVIEW
3. ACCOUNTANT_CHECKPOINT
4. LEGAL_ENTITY_SELECTION
5. CNPJ_FORMALIZATION
6. LOCAL_REGISTRATION_AND_INVOICING_REVIEW
7. PJ_BANK_ACCOUNT
8. ACCOUNTING_AND_TAX_CALENDAR
9. BRAND_AND_IP_PROTECTION
10. CONTRACT_LIBRARY
11. LGPD_AND_SECURITY_BASELINE
12. FINANCIAL_SEPARATION_AND_CONTROLS
13. OPERATING_READY
14. GROWTH_THRESHOLD_REVIEW

## AION teaching duties

For each stage AION should:
- explain the concept in plain Portuguese;
- show prerequisites;
- show documents/evidence normally needed;
- distinguish what can be prepared internally from what requires accountant/lawyer/official portal;
- keep a checklist;
- record completion evidence;
- warn about missing or stale evidence;
- refuse to claim legal/tax completion without confirmed evidence.

## Brand and intellectual property

The runway must cover:
- AtlasQuant brand/name protection;
- AION brand/name protection;
- software/code ownership;
- source-code and contractor IP assignment;
- software registration considerations;
- domain names and official digital identities;
- approved use of logos, names and commercial material.

No protection claim may be marked complete without confirmed evidence from the relevant official or professional process.

## Contract governance

AION may:
- draft contracts from approved templates;
- fill customer/company variables;
- summarize clauses;
- compare versions;
- highlight unusual or risky clauses;
- prepare negotiation notes;
- prepare an email package for sending after approval.

AION may not:
- provide an invented guarantee of legal validity;
- sign on behalf of HUMAN_OWNER;
- accept non-approved clauses;
- commit special pricing or liability;
- send the final contract externally without the applicable authorization policy.

Production templates should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being treated as standard approved templates.

## Email and external communications

AION may prepare and, once the appropriate communication envelope is authorized and connected, send routine approved communications.

Sensitive messages involving legal commitments, contract acceptance, exceptional pricing, confidential data or dispute positions require HUMAN_OWNER review.

## LGPD and security

The runway must include:
- data inventory;
- purpose and minimum-necessary data review;
- access controls;
- customer/tenant isolation;
- retention and deletion rules;
- incident-response contacts;
- vendor/integration review;
- confidentiality handling;
- audit evidence.

## Financial separation

Before meaningful commercial scale, the operating model should separate:
- personal money;
- business revenue;
- business expenses;
- taxes/provisions;
- reserves;
- approved development/marketing budgets.

AION should show cash position and obligations without inventing tax liabilities.

## Growth reviews

AION must periodically trigger a review when business conditions change materially, including:
- revenue growth;
- new activities;
- new employees/contractors;
- higher transaction volume;
- new state/municipal presence;
- foreign customers or payments;
- new regulated/sensitive data;
- new contractual risk.

AION must not assume a tax or legal framing remains appropriate forever.

## Specialist handoff

AION should generate a concise handoff pack for accountant/lawyer containing:
- current business model;
- revenue model;
- activities;
- customer types;
- questions needing validation;
- documents/evidence already available;
- decisions required.

## Fail-closed invariants

- no fabricated government/legal/tax status;
- no automatic legal filing;
- no automatic tax election;
- no contract signature;
- no unreviewed production legal template;
- no mixing personal and business bookkeeping in AION records;
- no claim of trademark/software protection without evidence.

This module is governed by AION but remains outside the frozen AION Core V1. It is subordinate to completion of official Core closure.
