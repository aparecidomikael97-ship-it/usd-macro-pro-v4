# AION Owner Formalization, Legal & Governance Runway V1

Status: DESIGN-ONLY / FAIL-CLOSED / REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL REVIEW FOR LEGAL-TAX EXECUTION

## Objective

Guide HUMAN_OWNER from the current pre-revenue/pre-formalization stage to a legally and operationally structured AtlasQuant/AION business without leaving avoidable gaps behind.

This runway is governed by AION but remains outside the frozen Core implementation surface. It composes with Core identity, authority, receipts and audit boundaries.

## Operating principle

AION must teach before asking HUMAN_OWNER to decide.

For each stage AION should:
1. explain what the item means in plain Portuguese;
2. explain why it matters;
3. show the available options;
4. identify what depends on current law/regulation;
5. retrieve or request fresh official evidence before execution;
6. identify when an accountant, lawyer, bank, INPI or public authority is required;
7. record the decision and evidence;
8. block progression when a prerequisite is unresolved.

## Formalization path

1. Define the actual business activities.
2. Validate CNAE/activity classification.
3. Check whether MEI is legally compatible with the intended activities and current official occupation list.
4. Compare MEI, Empresário Individual, Sociedade Limitada Unipessoal and other relevant structures with a qualified accountant.
5. Validate business name/viability with the competent registration path.
6. Register CNPJ using the applicable official process.
7. Complete municipal/state registrations when applicable.
8. Configure invoice issuance.
9. Open dedicated PJ banking/payment rails.
10. Define bookkeeping/tax calendar and responsibilities.
11. Establish a growth-threshold review so the company does not remain in an inappropriate regime as revenue expands.
12. Keep personal and business finances separated.

Current legal/financial thresholds must never be hard-coded as permanent truth. AION must re-verify official sources before HUMAN_OWNER acts.

## Employment coexistence

The formalization guide must explicitly explain that a CLT employment relationship may coexist with business ownership/MEI when legally permitted, but the consequences for benefits, labor characterization and tax obligations must be checked in the current official rules and, when material, reviewed by a qualified professional.

## Brand and intellectual property

AION must maintain an IP checklist for:
- AtlasQuant name/brand;
- AION name/brand;
- logos and distinctive visual identity;
- software/program-of-computer registration strategy;
- domain names and primary social handles;
- code ownership;
- third-party/open-source licenses;
- contractor/employee IP-assignment clauses;
- documentation of authorship and creation history.

Trademark protection is treated separately from software registration.

Before filing a trademark application, AION should help prepare:
- owner/applicant identity;
- candidate mark;
- relevant goods/services classes;
- search/evidence pack;
- approved filing scope;
- filing/monitoring checklist.

AION must not claim a mark is available or registered without official evidence.

## Contract library

AION should support an owner-approved contract library with versioning and provenance.

Initial templates may include:
- NDA/confidentiality;
- diagnostic engagement;
- pilot/POC;
- service agreement;
- recurring managed-service agreement;
- data-processing/privacy clauses;
- SLA/support annex;
- implementation scope/SOW;
- commercial proposal;
- IP/license clauses;
- termination/change-control clauses.

Before any template becomes a production standard it should pass qualified legal review.

## Contract drafting workflow

AION may:
- generate a contract draft from an approved template;
- populate company/client data;
- adapt scope, deliverables, SLA and annexes within approved policy;
- summarize the entire contract in plain Portuguese;
- explain each material clause;
- compare two versions and highlight changes;
- flag unusual liability, exclusivity, automatic-renewal, penalty, IP, privacy, payment or termination terms;
- identify missing fields/evidence;
- prepare a negotiation brief.

AION must not:
- sign;
- accept;
- waive rights;
- create unapproved legal commitments;
- silently replace an approved legal clause;
- represent a legal conclusion as certain when professional review is required.

Final legal acceptance remains HUMAN_OWNER or other duly authorized human.

## Email and document delivery workflow

After an approved final document exists, AION may prepare an email to the client and, when the authorized mail connector and outbound policy are enabled, send the approved document.

Sending must use:
- confirmed recipient;
- approved attachment/version digest;
- approved message;
- delivery receipt/evidence where available.

AION must never infer delivery success from an attempted send.

## LGPD and security baseline

Before production customer data is handled, the business must have at minimum:
- purpose/data inventory;
- data minimization;
- access control/RBAC;
- retention/deletion policy;
- incident-response path;
- processor/controller role understanding where relevant;
- customer/vendor data-processing terms where required;
- secrets/credential protection;
- backups and recovery;
- auditability.

Small-business status must never be interpreted as exemption from privacy/security obligations.

## Finance and accounting baseline

AION should guide HUMAN_OWNER to establish:
- separate PJ account;
- documented owner contributions/withdrawals;
- invoice-to-receipt reconciliation;
- tax reserve;
- recurring-cost register;
- client revenue/margin tracking;
- accountant handoff package;
- cash-runway tracking;
- no commingling of personal and company money.

## Professional review gates

The following are explicit review gates:
- ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- INPI_FILING_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- BANK_KYC_REQUIRED
- TAX_REGIME_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- PRIVACY_SECURITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED

AION may prepare the material for these reviews, but cannot self-certify professional or governmental approval.

## Growth reviews

AION should trigger a review when:
- revenue approaches the current regime threshold;
- employee/contractor structure changes;
- a new business activity is added;
- a new state/municipality or international market is entered;
- paid media or new payment rails are introduced;
- a client requests exceptional legal/security terms;
- customer personal data scope materially increases.

## Current-source rule

For current Brazilian legal, tax, corporate, INPI, labor, banking and privacy facts:
- prefer official government/agency sources;
- record retrieval date/source;
- distinguish current rule from historical guidance;
- never rely on stale remembered thresholds for execution.

## Priority relationship

This runway is mandatory before commercial scale, but it must not contaminate or reopen the frozen AION Core architecture.

The Core closes first. Formalization/legal/governance modules then attach through governed interfaces.
