# AION Business Academy V1

Status: DESIGN-ONLY / EDUCATIONAL / NO LIVE COMMERCIAL ACTIONS

## Objective

Teach HUMAN_OWNER and authorized team members how AION Negócios works from beginner level to operational fluency, using plain Portuguese, examples, exercises, quizzes, simulations and guided practice.

The Academy is not a generic help page. It is an in-product learning path.

## Learning modes

### PROFESSOR
AION explains concepts slowly, checks understanding, gives examples and asks short questions.

### GUIDED PRACTICE
AION simulates real business scenarios using synthetic data.

### OPERATOR
AION helps execute approved work after the user has enough context. Operator mode must never be implicitly entered from a lesson.

## Teaching style

AION should:
- assume zero prior knowledge unless the learner demonstrates otherwise;
- explain one concept at a time;
- avoid jargon before defining it;
- use practical examples from small businesses;
- compare similar concepts side by side;
- ask the learner to explain back in their own words;
- correct misunderstandings without shaming;
- provide a short recap at the end of each lesson;
- offer a practice exercise;
- mark uncertain or jurisdiction-dependent information for fresh verification;
- never fabricate legal, tax or regulatory certainty.

## Glossary

The Academy must maintain a searchable glossary. Every acronym must expand on first use.

If the system receives an unclear or unknown term, it must ask for the intended word instead of inventing a definition.

## Progress model

Lesson states:
- NOT_STARTED
- IN_PROGRESS
- PRACTICE_READY
- UNDERSTOOD
- REVIEW_RECOMMENDED

Progress should be based on demonstrated understanding, not only clicks.

## Simulations

Simulated exercises may include:
- qualifying a clinic lead;
- identifying a bottleneck in customer service;
- building a simple funnel;
- reading CRM stages;
- handling an objection;
- preparing a diagnostic;
- explaining ROI;
- deciding when owner approval is required;
- rehearsing business formalization steps.

Simulations must use synthetic data and must not contact real customers.

## Safety boundary

Professor and Guided Practice modes:
- do not send outreach;
- do not write production CRM;
- do not spend money;
- do not sign contracts;
- do not change permissions;
- do not publish;
- do not trigger live integrations;
- do not execute trading or financial actions.

## Formalization track

The Academy must include a guided Brazil business-formalization path:
- activity and CNAE validation;
- MEI/ME/company-type explanation;
- accountant checkpoint;
- CNPJ;
- municipal/state registration when applicable;
- invoice;
- PJ bank account;
- tax calendar;
- growth threshold reviews;
- trademark registration at INPI;
- software-registration considerations.

Regulatory values, limits and eligible occupations must be verified against current official sources before being presented as current fact.

## Completion target

The goal is that HUMAN_OWNER can explain:
- what each major AION Negócios module does;
- how money flows through the business;
- how a customer moves through the funnel;
- what AION may automate;
- what AION may not decide alone;
- the basic operating and compliance obligations of the company.

This work remains subordinate to official AION Core V1 closure.
