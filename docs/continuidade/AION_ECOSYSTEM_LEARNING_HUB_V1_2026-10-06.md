# AION Ecosystem Learning Hub V1

Status: DESIGN-ONLY / OWNER-LEARNING / NO LIVE ACTION AUTHORITY

## Objective

Create one central learning surface inside AION that teaches HUMAN_OWNER the entire AtlasQuant ecosystem:
- Trader;
- Negócios;
- Investimentos;
- AION/Core;
- any future domain registered later.

This is the owner's academy and second-brain learning surface. It complements domain-specific training areas without duplicating them.

## Core experience

HUMAN_OWNER may say:
- "AION, hoje eu quero aprender Negócios."
- "AION, me ensina B2B."
- "AION, me explica CRM do zero."
- "AION, me ensina calendário econômico."
- "AION, me mostra o que é Order Block."
- "AION, desenha pra mim como isso funciona."
- "AION, faz um resumo do que eu ainda não domino."

AION should respond by opening the relevant lesson path and teaching interactively.

## Learning surfaces

Lessons may use:
- slide-style explanations;
- diagrams;
- annotated images;
- charts;
- timelines;
- comparison tables;
- worked examples;
- synthetic simulations;
- short quizzes;
- voice explanations;
- guided practice;
- recap cards;
- links to approved videos and internal training material.

The visual explanation must be generated from approved knowledge/evidence. AION must not invent a chart, validation or market fact merely to make a lesson look complete.

## Teaching modes

### PROFESSOR
Explain from zero, slowly, one concept at a time.

### VISUAL_EXPLAINER
Prefer diagrams, slides, annotated visuals and step-by-step flows.

### SOCRATIC
Ask short questions to verify understanding.

### GUIDED_PRACTICE
Use synthetic scenarios and exercises.

### REVIEW
Revisit weak topics and misconceptions.

### OWNER_ADVISOR
Help HUMAN_OWNER reason through a real decision without making the reserved decision on their behalf.

## Owner Advisor

When HUMAN_OWNER asks during a negotiation:
- "AION, o que você acha desse valor?"
- "Essa empresa vale a pena?"
- "Qual o risco desse contrato?"
- "Nossa capacidade aguenta esse cliente?"
- "Qual pacote faz mais sentido?"

AION should:
- gather the available evidence;
- separate known facts, estimates and unknowns;
- analyze margin, effort, capacity, risk, customer fit and strategic value when available;
- compare options;
- state confidence and missing information;
- recommend a next step;
- clearly identify any reserved owner decision.

AION may advise. It must not silently convert advice into contract acceptance, pricing exceptions, spending or other owner-only actions.

## Learning progress

Progress should reflect demonstrated understanding, not clicks.

For each topic AION should track:
- NOT_STARTED
- LEARNING
- PRACTICE_READY
- UNDERSTOOD
- REVIEW_DUE

It should be able to answer:
- what HUMAN_OWNER has learned;
- what remains weak;
- what should be reviewed next;
- what prerequisite is missing.

## Personalized owner path

The system should prioritize:
1. concepts HUMAN_OWNER needs for current work;
2. weak areas detected in conversation/practice;
3. prerequisites for upcoming decisions;
4. periodic review of high-impact concepts.

The goal is not to overwhelm with the whole ecosystem at once.

## Relationship to domain training

Domain-specific training pages remain useful for clients, teams and specialists.

The AION Learning Hub is different:
- it aggregates the entire ecosystem;
- it is personalized to HUMAN_OWNER;
- it preserves cross-domain context;
- it can switch from Trader to Negócios to Investimentos in one learning session;
- it teaches how the pieces connect.

## Safety boundary

Learning modes never grant authority.

In learning/simulation mode AION must not:
- send real outreach;
- place trades;
- move money;
- sign contracts;
- publish;
- mutate production CRM;
- change permissions;
- activate live integrations.

Owner Advisor may analyze live context when authorized, but execution remains governed by the appropriate operational policy.

## Example lesson

User: "AION, me ensina Order Block."

AION:
1. gives a plain-language definition;
2. shows a simple price-structure diagram;
3. marks the order-block area;
4. explains confirmation and invalidation;
5. contrasts it with FVG;
6. gives one synthetic example;
7. asks the user to identify the block;
8. explains the answer;
9. records understanding state.

## Completion goal

HUMAN_OWNER should progressively be able to:
- explain every major ecosystem module;
- understand what each dashboard and workflow is for;
- make better commercial, trading and investment decisions;
- recognize when evidence is insufficient;
- know what AION can do autonomously and what remains owner-only.

This work is stacked above AION Business Academy V1 and remains subordinate to official AION Core V1 closure.
