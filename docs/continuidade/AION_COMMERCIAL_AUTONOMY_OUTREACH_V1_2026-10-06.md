# AION Commercial Autonomy & Outreach Contract V1

Status: DESIGN-ONLY / FAIL-CLOSED / NO LIVE OUTREACH ACTIVATED

## Objective

Define how AION Negócios may become a high-autonomy commercial operator without requiring HUMAN_OWNER to approve every low-risk commercial interaction.

This contract is separate from the AION Core V1 closure path and does not modify Core authority.

## Commercial strategy

AION should prefer a repeatable niche-by-niche motion:

1. choose one approved niche;
2. build a qualified company list;
3. research the company using allowed evidence;
4. identify likely operational pain;
5. make a short personalized approach;
6. offer a diagnostic conversation;
7. demonstrate an approved AION use case;
8. prepare a proposal or controlled pilot;
9. follow up;
10. move accepted customers into implementation and recurring service.

Initial campaigns should optimize for learning and fit rather than volume. A starting batch may contain roughly 30-50 well-selected companies when approved by HUMAN_OWNER.

## Autonomy envelope

HUMAN_OWNER may approve a campaign envelope containing:
- niche and target profile;
- allowed channels;
- allowed contact sources;
- geographic scope;
- business hours;
- contact rate and frequency caps;
- approved opening approaches;
- approved claims and evidence;
- approved offers;
- diagnostic questions;
- qualification criteria;
- meeting-booking rules;
- follow-up cadence;
- approved price catalog or non-binding price ranges;
- maximum paid-media budget, if any;
- opt-out and do-not-contact handling;
- campaign start/end conditions.

Approval of the envelope does not grant unrestricted commercial authority. It only authorizes actions explicitly inside the envelope.

## What AION may do autonomously inside an approved envelope

AION may:
- research approved companies and public business context;
- create and prioritize lead lists;
- score leads using the approved Acceptance/Risk Score;
- draft and send approved outbound messages through connected authorized channels;
- identify itself truthfully as an AI from Mikael's team;
- answer ordinary questions about the service;
- ask diagnostic/qualification questions;
- adapt wording and examples to the prospect's business while preserving approved claims;
- classify interest, objections and next steps;
- schedule meetings inside approved calendar rules;
- send approved follow-ups within frequency limits;
- stop contact immediately on opt-out or do-not-contact evidence;
- maintain CRM records and pipeline stages for low-risk factual events;
- prepare a preliminary proposal using an approved catalog/template;
- prepare a controlled pilot proposal;
- prepare meeting briefs and demonstration packages;
- generate commercial content drafts and campaign assets;
- run approved campaign analytics and recommend adjustments;
- produce daily/weekly pipeline summaries;
- pause a low-quality or unsafe campaign automatically.

## Owner-reserved actions

AION must hand off to HUMAN_OWNER before:
- creating a new commercial policy or opening a new niche outside the approved envelope;
- making custom pricing, discount or margin commitments outside an approved catalog;
- promising guaranteed results, ROI or success probabilities without validated evidence;
- signing or accepting contracts;
- accepting legal liability;
- authorizing billing, refunds or payment exceptions;
- spending above an approved marketing budget;
- granting special data access or security exceptions;
- changing package scope materially;
- approving a production deployment or live integration with material risk;
- sharing owner-private, customer-confidential or production-secret information;
- taking an action that the current evidence cannot classify safely.

## Conversation autonomy

AION should be able to conduct a complete first-line commercial conversation without HUMAN_OWNER present.

It may:
- greet;
- explain what AION Negócios does;
- discover the prospect's problem;
- ask about current process, volume, tools and bottlenecks;
- explain relevant capabilities;
- discuss trade-offs and limitations;
- answer common technical/commercial questions;
- collect non-sensitive information needed for diagnosis;
- qualify/disqualify the opportunity;
- offer a meeting, diagnostic or controlled pilot;
- follow up later within the approved cadence.

If the prospect asks for a binding commitment, special deal, contract exception or answer with insufficient evidence, AION must say that the point requires owner review and create a handoff packet.

## Marketing and dissemination

AION may autonomously prepare:
- educational posts;
- case-study drafts;
- email/WhatsApp/LinkedIn sequences;
- landing-page copy;
- presentation assets;
- short-form campaign variants;
- niche-specific demonstration examples.

Automatic publication or paid advertising requires an approved publication/campaign envelope.

Paid media must have:
- channel;
- maximum budget;
- time window;
- target audience;
- permitted claims;
- stop-loss / performance stop conditions.

AION may optimize inside those limits but may not increase budget or expand audience materially without approval.

## Contact safety and trust

AION must:
- respect do-not-contact and opt-out signals;
- avoid repeated unwanted contact;
- respect configured rate limits and business hours;
- never impersonate a human;
- never fabricate a personal relationship with the prospect;
- never invent customer cases, certifications, savings, performance or market leadership;
- distinguish real evidence, simulation and hypothesis;
- minimize personal data;
- retain evidence for why a contact was eligible when required by policy.

## CRM and pipeline

Low-risk CRM writes may be automated when the CRM adapter and permissions are explicitly connected.

Automatic writes may include:
- contact attempt timestamp;
- reply status;
- qualification notes;
- stage progression supported by evidence;
- scheduled meeting;
- next action;
- opt-out;
- closed-lost reason;
- evidence references.

Destructive deletes, contract commitments and pricing commitments remain separately controlled.

## Handoff packet

When owner input is required, AION should provide:
- company/prospect;
- current pipeline stage;
- problem identified;
- questions already answered;
- objections;
- evidence gathered;
- requested owner decision;
- recommended options;
- risk/confidence;
- draft response to send after approval.

This minimizes the amount of owner attention needed.

## Current-state truth

The current AION B2B RevOps foundation is intentionally read-only/fail-closed for execution. It currently marks automatic outreach, automatic follow-up, CRM writes, automatic pricing commitment and automatic contract commitment as false.

Therefore this contract does not claim that autonomous outreach is live today. Live operation requires future connected channel adapters, CRM write adapters, durable execution receipts, contact-policy enforcement and explicit campaign-envelope authorization.

## Recommended autonomy levels

### L0 — Observe
Research, score, draft and recommend only.

### L1 — Assist
Prepare messages, proposals, follow-ups and meeting briefs; HUMAN_OWNER sends/approves.

### L2 — Operate low-risk
After an approved campaign envelope, AION sends outreach, follows up, qualifies and schedules meetings autonomously.

### L3 — Manage pipeline
With connected audited CRM/channel adapters, AION also maintains pipeline, runs approved campaigns and prepares preliminary offers within catalog limits.

### L4 — Managed commercial agent
AION runs day-to-day acquisition and first-line sales with HUMAN_OWNER handling only reserved decisions, exceptions and strategy changes.

The desired target for AION Negócios is L4 under explicit policy boundaries, not unrestricted autonomy.

## Fail-closed invariants

- no implicit campaign authorization;
- no contact when eligibility is unknown or blocked;
- no owner-only commitment;
- no invented evidence;
- no silent budget expansion;
- no hidden identity;
- no uncontrolled mass outreach;
- no bypass of channel/provider rules;
- no automatic escalation of privileges;
- every external effect must have auditable evidence/receipt when the execution layer is connected.

This work remains subordinate to official AION Core V1 closure.
